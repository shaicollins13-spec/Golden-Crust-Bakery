About Golden Crust

A neighbourhood bakery concept built around fresh products, approachable service and a welcoming customer experience.

Our Story

Golden Crust Bakery is a hypothetical Pretoria small business created for this student project. The concept focuses on fresh bread, pastries, cakes and breakfast treats.

The website is designed to give customers one clear place to discover products, learn about the bakery and make enquiries.

Our Mission

To provide fresh, approachable baked goods and friendly local service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted neighbourhood bakery known for quality, consistency and welcoming customer service.

Our Values

  • Quality: Keep the focus on fresh, appealing bakery products.
  • Consistency: Create a dependable customer experience.
  • Friendliness: Make customers feel welcome and comfortable.
  • Community: Serve the needs of local residents and small celebrations.
